A measurement includes the original signal, selected band, spectral estimate and acquisition limitations.

  • Use a stationary camera and a textured region with in-plane motion.
  • Frequencies above half the camera frame rate can alias into the result.
  • Physical units require a scale in the same plane as the selected region.
How to interpret this measurement +

VibeScope estimates image-plane translation from the original frames. A separate stationary reference region can remove shared camera translation. Rotation, parallax, rolling shutter, compression, poor texture and lighting changes can still contaminate the signal.

The displayed frequency is a dominant observed response, not proof of a structural resonance. Damping fits require a free decay. Reported uncertainty describes the estimator or spectral resolution, not a traceable calibration certificate.

The bundled demo and model training use controlled synthetic motion. Real-world displacement accuracy and diagnostic reliability require independent experimental validation.
